# The Mahalaverse journey map: free, contribute, paywalled

Draft v0.1, 31 October 2026. Written for Meredith, Chris and Mick to agree before anything changes in the game.
Everything marked **Decision** is open. Figures marked *working* are the ones in the code today, not final.

## The idea in one paragraph

Nobody donates in the Mahalaverse: they co-create. The way in is free, and every space along the way has a literal gate.
At each gate you can look inside, taste one thing for free, and see everything else the space holds. To go all the way in
you either give (money, through the gate) or offer something of your own making (a poem, a meditation, a DJ set, a piece
of art). What people offer makes the world richer for the next visitor, and what people give builds The Seed on the playa.
Your account remembers all of it: your gates, your offerings, your badges, your application and your payments, so the
world opens itself to you as you take part.

## Three ways money and value move

| Door | What it is | Where it happens | Why there |
|---|---|---|---|
| Public donations | Any amount, no gift, no access | BackaBuddy (The Seed campaign) | Trusted, public, shareable. BackaBuddy's terms do not allow rewards for donations, so gifts and access cannot go through it |
| Gates | A set amount that opens a space and, from Gate 2 up, reserves a gift | PayFast, from inside the Mahalaverse | The payment comes back to the account automatically, so the gate opens by itself |
| Camp fee | A place in the camp, in full or in instalments | PayFast (card, or card subscription for instalments) or EFT with a reference | Only for invited or accepted members |
| In-kind offerings | A creative contribution | The Mahalaverse, stored with the account | Opens the lower gates (1 and 2, and the pods) without money |

The public total lives on the website (the Mahalaverse and the front page): BackaBuddy plus what the gates and the camp
put towards The Seed. BackaBuddy's story points people to the Mahalaverse and says where the full total is.

## Money is always shown in both currencies

Most givers are abroad, a large share are in South Africa. Every amount (a gate, the target, the total raised, a camp fee)
shows both, with the visitor's side first:

- Visitor abroad: **$100** (about R …, at the month's rate)
- Visitor in South Africa: **R …** (about $100)

How "visitor's side" is chosen: the account's "where do you live" answer if we have it, otherwise the browser's region,
with a small toggle (R / $) the visitor can flip. The rate is fixed by the camp once a month in one setting, so a gate
does not change price day to day. PayFast always charges in rand; the page says so beside the button.

**Decision:** gates are priced in dollars (and converted to rand), or set in rand with round numbers per currency
(a round dollar figure and a round rand figure, each set by hand). Round numbers in both read better; a single source currency is simpler to run.

## Who is looking: the account levels

One account, the same on the camp page and in the Mahalaverse, signed in by an email link (no password).

| Level | How you get it | What it changes |
|---|---|---|
| Guest | Not signed in | Free path, every gate's outside view and free taster. Nothing is saved beyond this browser |
| Builder | Signed in | Progress, card, book, quests and badges are kept. Can offer in kind and give through a gate |
| Invited | Your sign-in email (or your letter's private link) matches the invitation list | The camp door says "You are invited": apply and pay straight away |
| Applicant | Applied without an invitation | "You are on the waitlist": the camp contacts you for a call |
| Camp member | Invited or accepted, fee paid or a plan started | **Decision:** all spaces up to and including the zome (Gates 1 to 4) open, plus the crews and the build yard |
| Gate N giver | Paid through gate N | Every space up to gate N opens. Gates are cumulative: Gate 4 includes 1, 2 and 3's spaces |
| Offering maker | Made an approved offering | Opens Gate 1, or Gate 2 for the offerings listed below |
| Camp lead / steward | Set by a steward | Everything, plus the stewards' tools (offerings to read, applications, invitations, refunds) |

Your level is worked out on the server from what actually happened (a payment PayFast confirmed, an invitation on the
list, an offering a steward saw), never from what the browser says. Today the game keeps the gate in the browser, so this
is the main thing to build.

## The way, stop by stop

The seven stops already in the game, in order, with what is free, what can be offered and what is behind the gate.

### Gate 0. The invitation (the Book and the card, the portal): free

- **Free:** the teaser film, the story, the tour of the playa, the Book of Mahala, drawing your archetype card, walking
  through the portal.
- **Offer (optional, opens nothing yet):** the reflection prompt in your book ("What called you here?").
- **Nudge:** "Make an account to keep your card and your path." Signing in is asked for here, gently, not forced.

### Gate 1. The fire, in Blissfull Drift: $10 (working)

- **Free taster:** sit by the fire and read one poem by a camp mate (the fire chooses it).
- **Behind the gate:** every poem and story at the fire, "Let the fire choose" as often as you like, leaving your own
  line for the fire, the fire's audio.
- **Open it with an offering instead:** a poem or story of your own for the fire, or a journal reflection on
  "What does a temple mean to you?"
- **Gift:** the Seed giver badge in Your path, and the camp's thanks. No physical gift.
- **Decision:** drawing a card is free at the threshold today. Keep it free there and make the fire's own reading
  (a card drawn for you, with a question to sit with) the paid experience, or move card drawing behind Gate 1.

### Gate 2. The Lotus Mahal (and the meditation pods): $100 (working)

- **Free taster:** step inside and listen to one track (Sunrise at the Mahal), and see the coil.
- **Behind the gate:** sound journey recordings, guided meditations and breathwork from the community, the live online
  workshop with a guest speaker (link shown only to people inside), the six copper meditation pods around The Seed.
- **Open it with an offering instead:** a guided meditation, a breathwork session, a sound journey recording or a DJ
  set for the main tent.
- **Gift (waitlist):** a copper coil pendant, or a Seed T-shirt (the top view of The Seed at night). The T-shirt is
  made on demand: it shows as "on the waitlist" and is produced once enough are reserved.
- **Decision:** whether the pods sit with Gate 2 or get a gate of their own.

### Gate 3. The theme camp: invite only

Not something you buy. This gate is the camp's front door and it matches the camp page exactly.

- **Free taster:** meet the camp: photos, the main tent playlists, the Sacred Agreements, what a day looks like.
- **If you are invited:** "You are invited" with your letter, then the application form, then payment (in full or in
  instalments), all in one go.
- **If you are not invited:** "The camp is invite only. Apply, and we will be in touch." The same application form;
  the answer goes to the waitlist, and a camp lead books a call. Payment details appear only once you are accepted.
- **Shown amount:** the camp fee as a range, both currencies, marked working (the camp page today has R 7 500 for
  South Africa and $1 000 from abroad; the gate panel says $500). **Decision:** the one figure or range to show.
- **No offering opens this gate.** Build crew time can earn a discount code on the fee instead (see Gate 4).
- **Behind it, for members:** the member home (checklist, documents, dates), the kitchen roster, crew sign-ups.

### Gate 4. The zome: $1,000 (working)

- **Free taster:** one look inside The Seed in daylight.
- **Behind the gate:** the zome at every hour and lit at night, the crews and their quests, the build kitchen, the build
  film and the build guideline.
- **Offering:** none opens this gate. Building with us does: two weeks on the build crew earns a camp fee discount
  code, set by a steward.
- **Gift (waitlist):** a big coil, an art piece cut on the CNC router, or a Temple Tech light box.

### Gate 5. Honorary builder: $10,000 (working)

- **Behind the gate:** every space, including the build yard and the workshop.
- **Gift (waitlist):** a pendant, a CNC-cut art piece, a big coil and a light box, and the honorary builder badge.

### Gate 6. Cosmic architect: $50,000 (working)

- Everything below, the view beyond the zome to the temple of 2030, and the cosmic architect badge. No physical gift.

### Gate 7. The lighthouses: $100,000 and up (working)

- Everything below, the vision of lighthouse temples, and the lighthouse badge. No physical gift.

## Gifts: a waitlist with a delivery window

Every physical gift is a reservation, not stock on a shelf.

- At the gate: "Your pendant joins the waitlist. Expected [delivery window]." **Decision:** the window per gift.
- On the receipt and in Your path: the gift, its place on the waitlist, and its status (reserved, being made, sent).
- In plain words, once, near the button: about half of what you give makes your gift, the rest goes to building
  The Seed.
- The camp sees a waitlist per gift (how many pendants, which T-shirt sizes, where they go) so production can start
  when the numbers make sense.

## What the account tracks

For each person, in one place: invitation status, application and its stage, consent (and the date), payments and
pledges, camp fee plan and instalments, gates open and why (paid, offering, membership, steward), offerings made and their
review status, quests, shares (people who signed up through their link), badges, and gifts reserved and sent. The
person sees all of it in Your path and can download or delete it.

## The beta: one gate first

Before every space gets a paywall, run the whole loop on one: **Gate 1, the fire in Blissfull Drift.**

1. A guest sits by the fire and reads one poem free.
2. The gate shows the other poems as closed pages, with two buttons: "Offer a poem to the fire" and "Give $10 (R …)".
3. Either one opens the fire for good, on any device, once signed in.
4. Camp leads and ten friendly testers try both routes; we watch where they stop.

When that loop feels effortless, the same pattern goes on Gate 2, then the rest.

## Open decisions, in one list

1. Gates priced in dollars, rand, or round numbers in each.
2. What a camp member opens (proposed: Gates 1 to 4, crews and the yard).
3. Card drawing: free at the threshold, paid at the fire, or both.
4. Pods: part of Gate 2, or their own gate.
5. The camp fee figure or range to show at Gate 3.
6. Delivery windows for the pendant, T-shirt, big coil, CNC piece and light box.
7. Whether an offering opens its gate as soon as it is sent (with a steward able to take it back), or only once a
   steward has read it. Proposed: as soon as it is sent.
8. What counts towards the public total: everything given, or only what goes to the build (about half of a gate with a gift).
9. The front page line "The Mahalaverse is open ... Freely" now meets paywalls. Proposed: "The way in is free. Some
   spaces open with a gift of money, or of your own making."
