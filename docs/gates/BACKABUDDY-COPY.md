# BackaBuddy: words that connect the campaign to the Mahalaverse

Draft v0.1, 31 October 2026, for Meredith to post on The Seed campaign. Two pieces: a paragraph for the end of the
campaign story, and the first campaign update.

BackaBuddy stays the place for public donations of any size. It does not carry gates, gifts or camp fees: its terms do
not allow rewards in return for donations, so anything with a gift attached lives in the Mahalaverse.

## 1. A closing paragraph for the campaign story

> **Give here, or step into the Mahalaverse.**
> Every rand given here goes to raising The Seed. If you would like to go further, the Mahalaverse at
> wearemahala.com is the world we are building around the temple. Walk the way to The Seed, sit by the fire, draw your
> card, and offer a poem, a meditation or a set of your own. Its gates open spaces of the world, and some come with a
> gift made by the camp. The total on wearemahala.com counts this campaign and the gates together, so you can see
> everything the community has raised.

## 2. The first campaign update

> **The Seed has a world around it.**
>
> When we started this campaign we said we are not looking for donors but for temple builders. Here is what we meant.
>
> The Mahalaverse, at wearemahala.com, is a living map of The Seed and the camp around it. You can walk it from the
> Book of Mahala at the threshold, through the portal and the camp boat, all the way to the temple. Along the way are
> gates. Behind each one is a space: poems and stories by the fire, sound journeys in the Lotus Mahal, the inside of
> the zome.
>
> You can open a gate by giving through it, and some gates come with a gift we make by hand, like a copper coil
> pendant. Or you can open the first gates with something of your own making: a poem for the fire, a guided
> meditation, a DJ set. What you offer becomes part of the world for the next person who walks in.
>
> Donations here on BackaBuddy go straight to the build, and they are just as welcome. The full total, this campaign
> and the gates together, lives on wearemahala.com.
>
> Come and walk the way. [link to wearemahala.com]
