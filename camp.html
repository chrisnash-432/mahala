What's included with our camp

  • One main dinner a dayEaten together, and water for the week (seven litres a day).
  • A bell tent to rentPitched before you arrive (or bring your own).
  • Your coilEvery member wears one: a copper coil wound on the same grooved ring as the temple's heart, a small piece of the temple that carries its pattern.
  • The communal spaceThe kitchen, a chill and cuddle-puddle zone, long dining tables, a piano.
  • Activations through the weekThe fireside at the Blissful Drift (the camp's boat), the Lotus Mahal (our temple tent), sound journeys, the theatrical dinner, and whatever you bring to it.
  • Building The Seed togetherThe camp's art piece.
  • Incredible people and epic vibesCommunity calls through the year, build days in Cape Town, the camp circle on the playa, and the Great Unpack in Cape Town after the burn.

What you bring

  • Your own breakfast and snacks
  • More water, if seven litres a day is not enough
  • Camping gear and warm layers for the nights
  • Your AfrikaBurn ticket

Your AfrikaBurn ticket is bought separately, from AfrikaBurn, and is not part of the camp fee. Setup arrival Thursday 22 or Friday 23 April (to confirm). The burn runs Monday 26 April to Sunday 2 May at Quaggafontein. Strike Sunday 2 and Monday 3 May; everything comes home. Camp fees depend on where you travel from: you see your figure once you have told us, in one, two or four instalments.
